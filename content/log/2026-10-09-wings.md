We're giving engineers wings with AI and expecting everyone to fly.

But wings don't teach you how to fly. Curiosity does. That internal thrill of building something, breaking it, and figuring out why.

AI can make you faster, but it can't replace fundamentals or the desire to understand.

Everyone's busy building bigger wings. Not enough people are curious about how flying actually works.
